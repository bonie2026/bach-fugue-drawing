# Project Statement — working notes

The project translates J. S. Bach’s *Contrapunctus VIII* from *The Art of Fugue* (BWV 1080) into a generative drawing. Each note becomes a line, and the three voices share a circular timeline. Each voice has its own colour and translucent sheet. Viewed separately, the sheets reveal the movement of each voice. Aligned and layered, they show how the voices intersect over time.

The initial display idea used screen printing to explore colour overlap. The current plan uses three suspended translucent prints with a small gap between sheets, so their overlap changes with the viewing angle.
