# Background research — fugue and polyphony

- **Polyphony.** Multiple melodic voices unfold together. Each voice can be followed on its own, while its timing and pitch contribute to the combined texture.
- **Fugue.** A subject enters in one voice and is taken up by other voices as the music develops.
- **Contrapunctus VIII.** This movement from Bach’s *The Art of Fugue*, BWV 1080, is a triple fugue in three voices. The subjects are recurring musical ideas, and the installation layers correspond to the voices.
- **Connection to the drawing.** A shared time scale allows viewers to compare what the three voices are doing at the same moment.
